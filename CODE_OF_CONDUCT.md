# Code of Conduct — TérangaOS

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** April 2026  
**Language:** English

---

## Our Commitment

TérangaOS is an open source project for individuals wanting to leave Windows and reclaim control of their data. We create a **respectful, inclusive and professional** space for all contributors, regardless of their level of experience, background, gender, identity or orientation.

**We do not accept:**
- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Denigration or contempt
- Any form of intolerance

**We value:**
- Mutual respect
- Kindness
- Clarity and honesty
- Inclusion and diversity

---

## Expected Behaviors

### As a contributor

- Read the guidelines before contributing (this file, CONTRIBUTING.md, AI_POLICY.md)
- Be respectful in comments, issues and discussions
- Stay on-topic — use the right channels (GitHub issues, discussions, email)
- Test your code before submitting a PR
- Document your changes clearly
- Ask questions rather than assume

### As a maintainer

- Respond within 48 hours to each issue/PR
- Clearly explain change requests
- Politely reject misaligned contributions
- Review honestly without contempt
- Apply standards fairly

---

## Unacceptable Behaviors

**Immediately bannable:**
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Harassment (public or private)
- Publishing private data ("doxxing")
- Threats or violence
- Discrimination (race, gender, identity, orientation, etc.)

**Progressive warnings:**
- Spam or trolling
- Demeaning or insulting comments
- Systematically deviating from topic
- Refusal to respect maintainer decisions
- Systematic "toxic" behavior

---

## Reporting a Violation

**Confidentially and quickly:**

Email: conduct@teranga-os.org

**Include:**
- Precise description of the incident
- Link to comment/issue if possible
- Your contact for follow-up
- Preference: confidential or transparent

**Response times:**
- **24 hours:** Acknowledgement
- **7 days:** Preliminary decision
- **14 days:** Final decision

---

## Sanction Process

### Level 1: Warning
**Actions:** Trolling, repeated off-topic, isolated demeaning comments

**Consequence:** 
- Private notification to contributor
- Request to adjust behavior

### Level 2: Deletion + Warning
**Actions:** Abusive comments, light harassment, ignoring previous warning

**Consequence:**
- Comment/issue deletion
- Formal documented warning
- Possible temporary suspension (7-30 days)

### Level 3: Suspension
**Actions:** Repeated harassment, threats, discrimination

**Consequence:**
- Immediate suspension (1-3 months)
- Re-evaluation before reinstatement

### Level 4: Permanent Ban
**Actions:** Threats, violence, incitement, doxxing

**Consequence:**
- Immediate and permanent ban
- Deletion of all contributor's contributions
- Report to authorities if necessary

---

## Appeal Process

**If you disagree with a sanction:**

1. **Send an appeal request** to conduct@teranga-os.org within 30 days
2. **Explain your position** in detail
3. **Await decision** from an appeal commission (different from initial maintainer)
4. **Result:** Confirmation, revision or cancellation

**Appeal Commission:**
- 2 independent maintainers
- Community member (neutrality)
- Timeline: 14 days for decision

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Apply this code of conduct **fairly** without favoritism
- Document **all incidents** for transparency
- Respect **confidentiality** unless requested otherwise
- Recuse yourself if **conflict of interest**
- Review this code **annually** with the community

---

## Diversity & Inclusion

**We actively encourage:**
- Contributors from all countries (Africa priority)
- Contributors of all genders
- Contributors of all experience levels
- Contributions in French, English, Wolof, Pulaar, etc.
- Users wanting to leave Windows (African and international)

**If you feel excluded, speak up.** We want to fix it.
